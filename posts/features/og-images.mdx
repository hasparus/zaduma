For a looping video card, put a 1200×630 mp4 at `public/content/<slug>/og.mp4`
and point `img.ogVideo` at it. The build fails on paths outside `/content/`. The
generated image stays its still frame.

```yaml
img:
  ogVideo: /content/my-post/og.mp4
```

It emits `og:video` for Discord, Telegram, and iMessage. X needs a
`twitter:site` handle for player cards, so set `X_HANDLE` in
`src/lib/siteMeta.tsx`; then posts with a video also get an X player card that
loops it in `/og/player/`, and without it they keep `summary_large_image`.
