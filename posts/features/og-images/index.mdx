Posts get a card generated by `/api/og`, drawn over the header `img` or
`og.backgroundImage`. To use a ready-made 1200×630 image instead, put it next to
the post and point `og.image` at it.

For a looping video card, put a 1200×630 mp4 at `public/content/<slug>/og.mp4`
and point `og.video` at it. The OG image is its still frame.

```yaml
og:
  image: ./og.webp
  video: /content/my-post/og.mp4
```

This post has one. Share it on Discord or X to see it loop.

It emits `og:video` for Discord, Telegram, and iMessage. X needs a
`twitter:site` handle for player cards. `X_HANDLE` in `src/lib/siteMeta.tsx`
ships as the template author's, so replace it with your own handle, or set it to
`undefined` to drop `twitter:site` and keep `summary_large_image`. With a
handle, posts with a video also get an X player card that loops it on a
generated `/og/player/<post>/` page.
